The Atelier Story

Crafting Timeless symmetry

Architectural Origins

Timeless symmetry & organic fiber loops

Founded in the hearts of Milan and structured internationally, ROSH is a modern luxury fashion house committed to the absolute preservation of clean architectural symmetry, carbon-neutral master craftsmanship, and timeless, elegant silhouettes.

Every single collection piece is sculpted locally in limited micro-batches from 100% certified non-mulesed extra fine merino wool, double-faced Mongolian cashmere, and breezily breathable European flax linen under zero-waste guidelines. We design patterns intended to outlast seasonal trends, giving you a digital wardrobe defined by structural permanence.

01 / Master Tailoring

Handcrafted precision

All structural sleeves and collars are hand-sewn by local master tailors under clean zero-waste spaces.

02 / Organic Sourcing

Verified fiber weaves

Our sandwashed silk and organic flax flax require zero irrigation and are Oeko-Tex Standard 100 certified.

03 / Carbon Neutral

complimentary carbon-offset

every single order delivers wrapped inside signature recycled embossed boxes via fully carbon-offset transit loops.
